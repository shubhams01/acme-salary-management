# Product Assumptions

1. ACME manages approximately 10,000 employees.
2. Employees belong to different countries and departments.
3. Every employee has a unique employee code and email.
4. Each employee has one current compensation package.
5. Salary values are maintained in the employee's local currency.
6. Synthetic employee data will be used for development and demonstration.
7. HR Managers are authorized to access employee compensation data.
8. Salary revisions must preserve historical information.
9. Salary calculations are performed on the backend.
10. Analytics must not combine different currencies without conversion.
